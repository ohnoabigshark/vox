# Get Jack Back: Theme

*A pumpkin, a football team, and a long way home.*

This document is about who, where and what it feels like. It does not cover rules or numbers; those live in [GAMEPLAY.md](GAMEPLAY.md).

---

## The Pitch in One Breath

**Arcade Halloween Football.** **Jack**, a jack-o'-lantern, has lost his way home to the pumpkin patch, and he's asked a football team to help. They play through a haunted forest, a candy kingdom and a farm, one series at a time, past gnomes, scarecrows, owls and bats that don't want them there. You call the play, time the throw and break the tackles; everything else crashes together on its own. You start as seven plain, keen humans; series by series, the forest sends you recruits: a Golem built like a linebacker, a Werewolf that won't stop running, a Ghost that plucks passes out of the air. Keep going until a 4th down goes wrong.

**The feel:** snappy, fast, silly, and above all *physical*: every play ends in a pile-up. It's closer to *Crossy Road* and *Retro Bowl* than to *Blood Bowl*: pick it up in seconds, laugh every few seconds, one more go. **It's a portrait, one-thumb phone game in voxels**, to a cheerful Halloween soundtrack.

Inspirations: *Retro Bowl* (pocket-sized arcade football), *Crossy Road* (feel, readability, the chunky toy-like look), *Blood Bowl* (fantasy teams with clear roles), *Slay the Spire*, *Balatro* and *Dungeon Clawler* (start weak, build a run that snowballs), and the joy of a team of misfits that somehow works.

## The Story

**Jack is the pumpkin ball,** and he wants to go home to his patch. That's all the plot there is, and it's told in two tiny cutscenes:

- **At the start:** Jack bounces up to a football team and asks for help getting back to his patch. A few seconds, then the first snap.
- **At the end:** in front of the King, with the pumpkin patch behind him, your whole team (the field and the bench) dances around Jack: GOT JACK BACK.

The way home crosses three places, each with a Boss in a hat of its own: **Mister Frog** (a top hat) in Froggy's Forest, **the Queen** (a witch twice the size of any other, in a crown) in the Candy Queendom, and **the King** (a scarecrow in a crown) at Frederick's Farm, by the patch. Nobody explains why they're in the way; they just are.

## Core Fantasy

- **You call the play and make the throw.** You're Jack, the pumpkin who's also the quarterback: you read the defense, pick the play, and let yourself fly at the right moment.
- **You're getting Jack home.** Every Boss beaten is a step closer to the patch.
- **A plain team becomes a monster team.** You start with seven ordinary humans. By the end of a good run your lineup is a pack of misfits, each a trade-off: the Golem nobody can move, the Werewolf nobody can catch, the Ghost who catches everything.
- **Every recruit is a little story.** The Werewolf you picked up after the second series, who broke three tackles against Mister Frog, is *your* Werewolf.
- **Chaos you can read.** The defense is all on show, but four downs go fast. You call the play and adapt as it falls apart.

## Tone

- **Slapstick violence with no cruelty.** Players get flattened, punted and buried in leaves. A blocked pass bounces off a scarecrow's face. It's never sad for more than a second.
- **Snappy above all.** Quick downs, quick celebrations, quick restarts. Silliness happens in seconds, not in cutscenes.
- **Sports-broadcast energy.** Big calls on screen (GREAT!, FIRST DOWN!, TOUCHDOWN!), crowd chants, the stupidest moments made a fuss of.
- **Spooky, never scary.** It's a haunted forest at dusk, but the monsters are cute, the pumpkins grin, and the darkest thing in the game is a slightly sad tombstone.

## Art Direction

**Simple voxel art like *Crossy Road*, set in a haunted forest at dusk.**

- **Simple voxels.** Low voxel counts, chunky proportions and flat colors. No textures, no fine detail.
- **Haunted dusk, still readable.** A mossy, dark-green field, a leaf-strewn forest floor, fog in the trees, a dim moon, and the glowing pumpkin ball lighting the play. It's spooky, but the players on the field always pop.
- **Silhouette first.** Every player, opponent and hazard is recognizable from its shape and color alone, at phone size, from the game's camera behind the play. One shape, one rule, one telegraph.
- **The helmet is the team.** *If it wears our helmet, it's on our side.* Every teammate, human or monster, wears the team's football helmet and colors. The forest's opponents never wear helmets.
- **Two colors carry the readability.** Our team is **gold and orange**. The opposition is always **candy purple**. Nothing on the field should ever leave you wondering whose side it's on.
- **Destruction is cubes.** Hits burst things into colorful voxel chunks. No blood, ever.
- **Bodies turn and tumble.** Everyone faces where they're going and goes over when they're tackled; the pumpkin ball is the one thing that always faces you.
- **The overlay is retro pixel art:** chunky outlined panels, flat shadows, pixel portraits of each player, in the same palette as the voxels.

## Our Team

### Seven players and a pumpkin

**Your team is the QB and seven players: two Blockers, two Runners and three Catchers**, all in gold helmets and orange jerseys. Every human is built the same; what sets a player apart is its **kind**. They start as plain humans, keen and a bit useless, and line up again however many times they get flattened. Recruits who don't make the lineup wait on the bench.

### Monsters: the forest's recruits

**Every kind is a trade-off,** and every one keeps the team helmet on:

| Kind | Good at | Bad at | Suits | Look |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Human** | Everything, a little | Nothing in particular | Anywhere | A rookie in a helmet. |
| **Golem** | Heft: not moving when hit, flattening things | Speed | Blocker | A stack of mossy stones with glowing eyes, the helmet jammed on top; **the only one built like a linebacker**, wide and low. |
| **Werewolf** | Speed | Hands | Runner | Big, hairy, ears poking through the helmet, a snout and fangs. |
| **Ghost** | Hands: reach, plucking passes out of the air | Heft | Catcher | A white sheet with a helmet on top, hovering, unlit so it always reads. |

Later candidates: the Skeleton, the Mummy, the Pumpkin-head, the Black Cat, Frankenstein's Monster.

### The ball

**Jack, a jack-o'-lantern.** A round pumpkin with a glowing carved face: the team's ball, the game's hero and its icon. **There's no quarterback: the pumpkin throws itself.** It floats behind the line, bobbing, its face always turned to you, and fires itself off as a bullet. Embers pour off it (sparks and bigger flame chunks, flashing white-hot and cooling to red), trail behind it in flight, and burst out when it's thrown, caught or knocked down; in both cutscenes too. **It's also the field's light:** a warm lamp burns inside it, lighting everyone around it and throwing their shadows, so the shadows swing as it flies and pool around whoever is carrying it. The moon is only a dim fill.

## The Opposition

**The locals don't want a football team tramping through their land.** They're the defenders: wild creatures, never in helmets, always in candy purple. The Bosses are the one exception to "no hats": a top hat, a crown, a bigger crown.

### Froggy's Forest

| Defender job | Froggy's Forest local | Look |
|---|---|---|
| **Rusher** (the pass rush) | **Rhino Beetle** | Low, wide and horned, a little battering ram. |
| **Cover** (man coverage) | **Garden Gnome** | A tall pointy hat, a beard, arms out. |
| **Zone** (planted, guards a wide area) | **Scarecrow** | Arms spread wide along its crossbar, a literal wall. |
| **Safety** (the deep guard) | **Owl** | Wide-eyed, hanging deep, seeing everything. |
| **Bat** (patrols a box of sky) | **Bat** | Hovering with its wings spread, to knock passes out of the air. |

Plus the **Frog** (a Cover that crouches, then leaps) **Boss: Mister Frog,** who rains frogs down a lane.

### The Candy Queendom
The Queen's gingerbread realm: pink-and-white turf, gingerbread houses, candy canes and a few trees along the sidelines. **Witches take the Safety's place,** hanging deep on their brooms, and the **Gingerbread Man** is new: a flat cookie with icing eyes and smile, and the opposition's purple gumdrop buttons and bow tie. **Boss: the Queen,** a witch twice the size of any other, in a crown, whose **Royal Decree** bans one of your play cards each down (a red wax seal stamped BANNED across it).

### Frederick's Farm
A tilled farm field (brown soil, furrows running up the field, green sprouts) with dark soil beyond, fences and glowing jack-o'-lanterns along the sidelines, pumpkins and hay bales. Rows of scarecrows, and **Crows** (black, with dark purple feathers) flying lanes up and down the field. **The pumpkin patch is the last end zone**, and **the King**, a giant scarecrow in a crown, stands guard in front of it, never moving.

Each area has its own locals, hazards and look, and a Boss defense at the end of its four series. It's always the same team, growing as it goes.

## Attributes

Every player and every defender is described by **three attributes**, the **Three H's**, on a scale where **50 is ordinary**. They're physics, not dice (GAMEPLAY.md, *Your Team*):

| Attribute | What it means | Who's known for it |
|---|---|---|
| **Heft** | Weight: shoving, not being shoved, breaking tackles. | Golem, Rhino Beetle |
| **Hustle** | Speed. | Werewolf, Owl |
| **Hands** | Reach for the ball, and winning a contested one. | Ghost, Scarecrow |

## Title

**Get Jack Back**: getting Jack the jack-o'-lantern back to his patch. It's the game's name; *Arcade Halloween Football* is the pitch.

Runners-up, kept for reuse inside the game (perks, achievements, area names): *Fourth & Ghoul* (the old title), *Field of Screams*, *Hike & Shriek*, *Trick Play*, *Ghoul Line*, *Boo-tball*, *The Boo Zone*, *Haunted Huddle*, *Hail Mary Shelley*, *Fright Night Football*, *Touchdown Town*. *Fee Fi Fo Fumble* lives on as a crowd chant.
